### Hi there 👋

I'm a lecturer in computational atmospheric chemistry at the [University of Bristol](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/chemistry/), in the [Atmospheric Chemistry Research Group](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/chemistry/research/acrg/), where I lead a group working on atmospheric chemistry, chemistry-climate modelling and machine learning. My pronouns are he/him.

Funded projects in the [group](https://github.com/grif-lab) use [GEOS-Chem](https://geoschem.github.io/index.html), [UKESM1](https://ukesm.ac.uk) and develop [UKCA](https://www.ukca.ac.uk).

#### what I'm working on

- ⚗️ **Chemistry for [CliMA](https://clima.caltech.edu)**: My Julia journey continues via work in [ClimaAtmos.jl](https://github.com/CliMA/ClimaAtmos.jl) with Tapio Schneider's group at Caltech, and I'm interested in improving how interactive chemistry becomes affordable and calibratable in large ensembles.
- 🛰️ **Foundation models for air quality**: This year, I had a short fellowship to work on fine-tuning pretrained weather models to ground-station observations in Southeast Asia, working with Mohd Talib Latif and Maggie Ooi at Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia.
- 🔥 I'm enjoying working on a new project on interactive fire and dust chemistry in CESM, on Bristol and Isambard HPC, to scope contributions to [RAMIP](https://ramip.ncas.ac.uk/)
- 🌍 Work continues on a project about the [COP26 Global Methane Pledge](https://www.globalmethanepledge.org) 
- 🌍 **The [Global Carbon Project](https://www.globalcarbonproject.org) CO budget**: led by colleagues at UC Irvine and at JAMSTEC, where I have a visiting position.
- 🥳 I'm excited to be a co-I of the Schmidt Futures Virtual Earth System Research Institute [VESRI](https://www.schmidtfutures.com/our-work/virtual-earth-system-research-institute-vesri/) [FETCH4](https://fetch4.github.io) project focusing on the role of methane in the Earth system.  This project is led by [Alex Turner](https://github.com/alexjturner) (UW), [Lee Murray](https://github.com/ltmurray) and Vasily Vlasenko.
